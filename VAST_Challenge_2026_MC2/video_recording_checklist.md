# MC2 Video Recording Checklist

Use this file immediately before recording the final <=4 minute video.

## Required Setup

- [ ] Open the final interactive website: `rebuild/index.html` locally or the stable GitHub Pages URL.
- [ ] Use a 16:9 browser window, preferably 1920x1080 or 1600x900.
- [ ] Zoom level is 100% or adjusted so labels are readable.
- [ ] Microphone works and voice narration is recorded.
- [ ] Timer is visible to the presenter; target duration is 3:40-3:55.
- [ ] Do not rely on slides only; the official expectation is visual analytics software in operation.

## Required On-Screen Actions

- [ ] Start on Overview and show baseline: 185,147 events, 108 SaidIt posts, 3 `content_source` anomalies.
- [ ] Open Q1 and show the SwiftWren target chain for 2046-05-17 04:21.
- [ ] In Q1, click a terminal recipe step or relay point and show the evidence panel with raw event details.
- [ ] Open Q2 and switch among SwiftWren, MellowOtter, and HiddenOrca.
- [ ] In Q2, explicitly point out observed / inferred / unknown boundaries.
- [ ] Open Q3 and show the prior-incident timeline.
- [ ] In Q3, click a timeline marker or the gate evidence panel.
- [ ] Show the recommended single intervention: Agent + `content_source` at the SaidIt boundary -> block/review.

## Claims To Say Exactly

- [ ] "The logs show actions, filenames, actors, and timestamps; they do not show the exact file bodies."
- [ ] "John Windward Agent is the only observed terminal Agent endpoint completing these file-source SaidIt posts."
- [ ] "HiddenOrca source is unknown in the visible window."
- [ ] "The recommended intervention covers 3/3 known anomalies and 0/105 normal human SaidIt posts."
- [ ] "The intervention is one point only: the SaidIt publication boundary."

## Claims To Avoid

- [ ] Do not identify a human attacker as fact.
- [ ] Do not claim motive as fact.
- [ ] Do not claim exact leaked text.
- [ ] Do not claim HiddenOrca's original source document is known.
- [ ] Do not present multiple equal interventions; alternatives are comparison context only.
- [ ] Do not say the intervention blocks every possible future variant; note that direct `content` variants would need additional monitoring.

## Final Packaging After Recording

- [ ] Add a stable video URL to `index.htm`, or include `video.mp4` / `.wmv` if size permits.
- [ ] Test the video link in an incognito/private browser window.
- [ ] Confirm the final video duration is <=4:00.
- [ ] Rebuild the official zip after adding the video link/file.
- [ ] Do not include exploratory notes such as `Q AND A.md` in the official zip.
