Conclusion After EDA

answer supported by the figures above
Main finding. The anomalous post was produced by an Agent-mediated file-posting workflow, not by John manually typing a SaidIt message. The target post used content_source=SwiftWren.txt.
Detailed view. Emma Harbor's Agent read meeting_notes.doc, created SwiftWren.txt, and the related SwiftWren_further_instructions.md task propagated through queue_subordinate_task(task=read_file). The final relay reached John Windward's Agent, which executed saidit_post_check, saidit_post(content_source), and two cleanup deletes.
System overview. Normal SaidIt posts use human-written content. Only 3 of 108 SaidIt posts use Agent content_source, and SwiftWren is the largest observed relay chain among those incidents.
Important correction: SwiftWren reached John five times. The first four arrivals did not directly produce the public post in the immediate evidence window; the fifth produced the target post. Arrival at John is necessary in the observed terminal chain, but not sufficient by itself.
